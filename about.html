About Me

I'm Christina Wright, a junior at the University of Kansas studying Marketing with a Professional Selling certificate. At my core, I'm people-focused — I'd rather build a relationship than sit behind a desk, which is exactly why sales and marketing pulled me in. I love the moment when a conversation turns into a connection, and I'm looking for a career built on exactly that.

My faith is the heartbeat of my life, and it shapes how I show up for people — whether that's leading a weekly gathering of 50+ students through Met By Love, the ministry I founded on campus, or mentoring a group of 17 girls through Salt Company. I've also put that same people-first energy into business: as Chief Marketing Officer of a small startup, I built a product from the ground up and learned firsthand what it takes to turn an idea into something people actually want to buy.

Outside of class and leadership, you'll usually find me with the people I love — traveling, out on the boat, taking a walk, or catching up over coffee.
